# Lifestyle final polish

Baseline: published `439aa14` on `v2`. Final gameplay/UI source is `7fe1a2e1`.
The catalogue, artwork, navigation, asset feature boxes, purchases,
ordinary/enhanced world versions, reader 32 and archive formats remain unchanged.
The implementation was initially pushed at the user's request while verification
was still running. Those jobs survived the temporary cloud disconnection and
completed. Their provenance and the later UI-only follow-up are recorded below.

## Decisions and journals

New offers save `copyVersion:2`, `journalVersion:1` and `outputVersion:1`.
Their 120 choices each have an independently authored first-person journal,
second-person result and frozen result title. The engine owns the single journal
write; the activity record remains, without its additional fact. An injury still
creates its actual health condition, while suppressing only that new story's
extra symptom fact. Old medical actions retain their original journalling.

Missing revisions preserve the published duplicate-write and empty-effect
settlement literally. Existing journals and pending wording are never cleaned up
or reauthored. Exact replay fixtures independently captured from archived
`439aa14` cover all 120 earlier choices; the original first-stage fixtures remain.

## Actual result effects

Small subject snapshots compare resolved state before and after the bound choice.
The existing EventModal displays only changed, capped values: condition/value,
maintenance, satisfaction/relationships, pet happiness/bonds/training, actual
hobby proficiency or discipline/respect/happiness, practice attempts, real
injuries and real incident records. Costs and insurance credits come from new
ledger transactions and have distinct labels. An incident is never called a
conviction or court case. Comfort is never called healing.

Snapshot/display preparation is read-only and consumes no random draw. New
operation names `discipline` and `pet-comfort` describe their real effects;
earlier `competition` and `pet-health` aliases keep their saved interpretation.

## Driving ages and cash

One authored policy supplies road purchase, licence and usage ages for all twelve
countries. US/Canada use 16, UK/Australia 17 and the remaining countries 18; boats
and aircraft use 18. These are simplified game policies, not certification.
An eligible teen can buy a road vehicle with cash, review its activities, pass the
road test, drive and maintain it. The appropriate licence is still required.
Property purchases, vehicle gifts/scrapping, trade-in and other adult actions
keep their adult rules. Cash purchases use the actual available balance, leaving
locked inheritance untouched. No birthday grants a title, money, debt or licence.

## Gameplay and verification

The complete frozen invocation at `5f51d2e8` passed **392 suites / 6,959 tests**
in 2,347.579 seconds with zero failures, skips, todos or runtime/test source drift.
The final UI-only `7fe1a2e1` follow-up disables the adult-only trade-in entry and
replacement rows using the same adult eligibility helper as the resolver. Its
visible explanation is also included in the accessibility label. A separate
three-suite rerun passed 84 affected cases, and strict app/test TypeScript passed.
These are overlapping executions, not 7,043 unique tests or one complete run on
the later UI source. The 596-module acyclic import graph and notice checks pass.

The 120-life soak completes all seeds 0–119 with a largest retained ledger of
61,470 rows. The UI follow-up changes no game, persistence, policy or runner
source. Both final Android/iOS Hermes exports contain exact source matches for
all 16 changed runtime files, without missing/mismatched sources. This establishes
production bundling, not installed-device acceptance.

Eight policy journeys commit real available commands, wages and dates to on-disk
SQLite and physically reopen connections after every command. They cover an
under-18 earned-money driver, investment/tenancy/disagreement/maintenance/sale,
vehicle use/upkeep/gift, pet training/illness/family succession, paid hobby peers/
competition/projects, multiple generations, an ordinary enabled 20–90 control
and a paid-reading life ending naturally at 84. No mortality, funds, dates or
random streams are patched to force those outcomes. The latter has 13 new

policy exceeds two situations per calendar year. Total decisions/results include
manual actions and ordinary life events, distinguished from optional situations.
Actual costs, skill dates, save sizes and severity proxy are retained in the
