## Actual rule

Only new ordinary applications bind V1 experience terms and a per-answer chance.
Roles use their existing sector data: real shop work supports Retail Assistant,
and café work supports Barista. Each six distinct proved paid calendar months
adds one point before the existing chance bounds, at most five points for thirty
or more months. The factual new sentence says “can add up to” because the 95%
ceiling may reduce the effective bonus. It promises no hiring, rank or pay.

One shared evidence pass is used when binding the application and one when
validating its four answers. Hourly evidence requires an actual worked period
and the same person's exact positive gross-transaction link. Salaried evidence
requires a positive ordinary monthly wage receipt, matching currency and dates,
and an unambiguous contract after considering **all** competing contracts before
filtering sector, company or special roles. Those salaried payments prove paid
calendar months; they do not prove attendance or rule out paid hospital leave.
Calendar-month sets prevent double-counting multiple receipts/jobs. Amounts and
currencies do not turn into extra months.

The conservative V1 slice excludes company payroll, old supplied background
service, aggregate background income, back pay, pensions, missing receipts and
ambiguous contract-switch months. Exact hourly leave/hospital periods are also
excluded. Omitted evidence earns no invented retrospective benefit. Current
history counters, wages, accounts, qualifications and all employment facts remain
unchanged by deriving or binding the quote.

Original effects have no `experience` field. They retain the original choose-time
chance and original copy; no optional field is defaulted into them. New quotes
are checked against real evidence and current eligibility before resolution.
An actual answer still consumes one outcomes draw, withdrawal none. Application,
rendering, preview and codec reload consume none. Successful hiring still starts
at the existing role's rank-zero contract, with no upfront salary.
