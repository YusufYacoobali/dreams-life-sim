# Cult Leader

Cult Leader is an independent career under **Special Careers → Independent
Careers → Cult Leader**. It uses a main dashboard and one headquarters screen.
Other management actions use the existing Dreams selection, confirmation and
result popups. Life's current-context shortcut and occupation labels return
the active leader to the dashboard.

Founding is available to an adult outside custody, pursuit, hospital and travel
who can afford the reviewed local startup cost and has no other full-time
commitment. Part-time work remains possible. Starting another full-time job,
contract, course, service, campaign, recording, public royal duty, owner-operated
business or full-time creator studio requires leaving cult leadership first.
The organisation is tied to its headquarters city; moving abroad requires
disbanding. Passive business interests are not a second full-time commitment.

## Beliefs, people and stories

The founder chooses a name, title, one of eight philosophies, two doctrines,
two rules, premises, fees and launch method. Later belief changes allow two to
six distinct doctrines and rules from 28 of each. Every definition contributes
to bounded growth, loyalty, satisfaction, costs, donations or heat modifiers.
Changes have action limits and affect actual loyalty and member memories.

Ordinary followers are an aggregate. Three real named people form the initial
inner circle; deliberate recruitment can add two more at 80 followers. Each
retains their existing person ID, relationship, portrait, loyalty, ambition,
responsibility and bounded memories. Talking, praise, responsibilities, rewards,
confrontation and expulsion use actual people. Expelling an ambitious,
resentful member can schedule that same person to return 24 months later as a
rival leader. Repairing the real relationship can prevent the rival's return.
The feature creates at most five original inner-circle NPCs per organisation.
Only active seats count toward the five-person limit. Up to two paid invitations
per year can fill vacancies using actual known local adult contacts, including
at a full headquarters. Invitations never generate replacement NPCs. Former
members remain searchable with their identities, memories and consequences;
rejoining requires repaired trust and retains their original history. A person
cannot serve two active circles or found another organisation while serving one.
Assigning the same responsibility again is unavailable in both UI and engine;
multiple advisers can share that responsibility without displacing one another.

Routine recruitment, preaching and all six ceremonies have distinct outcomes
that depend on beliefs, public standing and available members. Sermons vary by
philosophy. Preparation, ambition, grievances, reputation and public visibility
matter; private setbacks do not automatically become public scandals. Sixteen
activity incidents can interrupt ordinary actions with immediate decisions,
such as a publicity sponsor demanding a prophecy or a public challenge at a
Grand Gathering. These incidents are generated by the relevant action, not by
the quarterly random-story pool, and respect the existing monthly scene limit.

There are 56 authored situations with 168 distinct choices: the original 28,
12 more dramatic situations and 16 activity incidents. They are gated on actual
rules, doctrines, responsibilities, family, finances, rivals or headquarters. Follow-up
threads can turn a hidden livestream into later recruitment, an accusation
into publicity, or a humiliation into a departure. Stories share the existing
pending-event queue and priorities. A withdrawal choice prevents a saved cult
scene from trapping the player when circumstances change.

Eighteen wild claims have saved dates, deadlines, witnesses, doctrine,
approach and promised outcomes. Some settle quietly. Important failures offer
a claim-specific admission, one twelve-month postponement, a distinct practical
remedy or withdrawal. Remedies include actual dues refunds, witness compensation,
a one-time kettle auction, a paid workshop and revised beliefs. Cash, participant
availability and free belief slots are checked before offering the action.
Quiet failures still settle quietly unless a public double-down or high heat
makes a decision necessary. Old saved offers retain their original
admit/postpone/blame choices and exact outcomes; only newly offered claims use the revised responses. Keeping an
appropriate ceremony promise records fulfillment at the actual successful
gathering; later events cannot erase it. Saved random outcomes do not reroll on
reload. A claim can be made only once per organisation, even after its old
thread is archived.

## Money and progression

The communal treasury is separate from every personal bank account and estate.
Founding pays actual registration and capital costs. Ordinary-member joining
and monthly fees, donations, ceremonies and workshop sales create recorded
income. Premises, administration, ceremonies, publicity, upgrades and member
support create recorded expenses. Named inner-circle members are exempt from
ordinary aggregate dues.

Five headquarters tiers and six upgrades change real capacity, appeal, running
costs, satisfaction, residence or merchandise income. Fees affect growth and
loyalty. Public heat affects recruitment, loyalty and story eligibility. Rivals
retain actual leaders and shared history and change monthly. Recovery takes
six processed months; a rebrand retains old names, scandals and legal cases.

Leader payouts require three processed months, no arrears, earned profit and
reserves for two running bills. The transfer records both a treasury debit and
personal taxable/support-relevant income. Named member rewards also credit the
actual recipient. Misappropriating real funds records both sides and enters
the ordinary fraud investigation, arrest and court pipeline; it is not an
extra free-money crime button.

Monthly accrual is guarded by the last processed calendar month. Arrears,
departures, rivalry, heat and scale can cause collapse. Disbanding is also
available from custody. Closure pays what bills it can, records remaining
organisational write-offs and distributes remaining cash outside the personal
estate. Death closes the cult with no automatic successor or inherited
communal balance. People, membership, financial totals and history remain.

## Persistence

Current saves use World 48 / engine 50 / content 27 / SQLite reader 28, database v4.
Published Cult Leader World 46 records remain literal. The concurrent writing
branch used the same World 46 markers without a cult collection; only that absent
field gains an empty collection during migration. Existing cult records are never
replaced, and malformed present values are rejected.
Published Polish 3 World 45 saves gain an empty cult history prospectively,
without consuming random draws. The World 44 → 45 Polish 3 migration and all
preceding mini-game, rental and personal-support branches remain supported.
World 46 cult saves and integrated World 47 saves advance their version markers
without rewriting members, threads, pending offers, random state or financial
history. Genuine pre-polish fixtures prove exact old offer outcomes across the
upgrade.
Reader 13–27 upgrades are metadata-only and do not rewrite stored lives,
receipts or checkpoints.

Commands are strict typed unions. Validation reconciles archived and recent
treasury books, personal transfer receipts, headquarters purchase history,
legal incidents, named identities and pending thread offers. Bounds include
50,000 followers, five active named members with retained former-member records,
six lifetime rivals, 72 threads, 512 recent
book rows with archived totals, 60 monthly reports, 120 annual reports and
18 lifetime claim IDs. No-cult months are a no-op with no new RNG consumption.

The dashboard keeps the core statistics compact. Doctrines and rules can be
searched by name or description while retaining the current selection. The
only two management routes remain the dashboard and headquarters.

All content and the original 12 Image Gen illustrations are bundled locally.
No runtime dependency or artwork was added in this polish phase. See

[original implementation verification](verification/cult-career/README.md) and

## Writing

The cult editorial pass covers all 56 situations, 168 choice payoffs and 18
claims, plus beliefs, routine activities, follower reactions and screen text.
Existing saved offers retain their original wording and resolution. New offers
use the revised copy without changing gameplay. See the
[43 before/after examples and verification](verification/cult-writing/README.md).

The follow-up [voice refinement](verification/cult-writing-refinement/README.md)
varies claim admissions, sharpens the humour, removes duplicate routine summaries
and casts present named members with personality-specific ordinary reactions.
