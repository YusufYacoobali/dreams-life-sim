# Observed development for all sixteen hobbies

These are 32 observations from sixteen independent employed-graduate cohorts,
with two real paid routines per cohort. No dates, funds, health or RNG were
patched. Each command commits to on-disk SQLite and cold-reopens it. The full
records, fees, threshold dates and actual payments are in
[hobby-development.json](hobby-development.json). Reproduce with
`node tools/measure-lifestyle-hobby-development.cjs OUTPUT.json`.

All community routines reached 90 proficiency after 45 attended and elapsed
months. All lessons required 23 attended months, but elapsed time was 30–49
months with this two-routine policy. Missed lessons were recorded as fatigue;
missed sessions charged nothing. This is a measured policy result, not a claim
that every player's routine has the same calendar pace. Dedicated rest and other
actual choices can change attendance. The per-attended-month gains remain the
unchanged 2/4, and no tuning was introduced to produce these observations.
