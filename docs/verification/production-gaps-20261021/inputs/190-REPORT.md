## Actionable finding

**P1 — an Italy work move can make the next monthly save invalid.** Italy's authored school assessment age is nineteen, while adulthood is eighteen (`src/game/rules.ts:36`). An eighteen-year-old who finished another country's school without a qualification and has no explicit withdrawal can request an ordinary full-time work offer to Italy: `offerReason` checks destination adult age and role requirements, but not the school enrolment the destination will apply (`src/game/systems/relocation.ts:54`, `:60`). Cleaner, Retail Assistant, Barista and Warehouse Assistant require no qualification (`src/game/content/jobs.ts:9`).

After an approved move, the work route installs a full-time job (`src/game/systems/relocation.ts:184`). If the actor's education was already `none`, moving does not change it (`:180`). In the next actual month, `educationMonth` auto-enrols anyone below destination `schoolEnd` without a secondary qualification or explicit withdrawal (`src/game/systems/education.ts:129`). The source processes education before monthly work (`src/game/engine.ts:123`). `validateWorld` then rejects the resulting full-time job plus secondary-school stage with `Invalid current employment` (`src/game/model.ts:198`). This is a concrete source-path conflict with the existing ordinary-job study guard (`src/game/systems/work.ts:58`), not a claim about Italian employment law. An eighteen-year-old current pupil moving between schooling contexts can encounter the same conflict immediately when the destination school is retained.

Recommended narrow correction: share or mirror the actual destination public-school enrolment predicate, and refuse an otherwise valid full-time work offer/move if the actor will still be enrolled there. Recheck at confirmation because an approved offer can outlive changes to the actor. Keep country ages, qualification rules and explicit withdrawal unchanged; do not create an implicit withdrawal or award a qualification. A focused reproduction should start with the genuine failed-school archive or a declared unit state, take the ordinary approved work move, and prove valid next-month progression. Include an explicitly withdrawn eighteen-year-old and a qualified eighteen-year-old as controls. Existing stored offer terms and history should remain intact.

This finding has been sent to the coordinator. Runtime reproduction and implementation await their task/scope decision.

## Checks without another actionable finding

- **All twelve country definitions:** school start precedes secondary start; every voluntary leaving minimum lies within secondary school and before normal assessment. The configured leaving ages are US/CA/GB/FR/DE/ES/IT16, AU/BR17, IN14 and JP/ZA15. All current adulthood gates are18 and ordinary part-time work starts at16. The gap between earlier voluntary leaving and later work/recovery eligibility is explicitly authored, not a grant of a job or instant qualification. Italy's normal assessment/recovery boundary remains19.
- **School withdrawal and recovery:** withdrawal binds the actual current community and country, refuses primary/below-minimum/custody/away contexts, retains incomplete history and does not award qualifications. Its recorded actor is exempted from inferred under-school-end student hours; actual later full-time study still restricts contracted hours, and evening recovery remains compatible with work. Recovery separately requires the later of adulthood and normal school assessment age.
- **Admissions prices:** all twelve frozen V1 annual quotes exactly equal the current country pack after native minor-unit scaling. JPY is600000minor, not60000000; all other currencies have two decimal places. Tuition validation binds the stored country, city, currency and V1 amount, and accepted-place confirmation rechecks actual prerequisites/current location. V1 pricing is explicitly an authored historical table; it does not claim national admissions rules. Lifecycle/history/aid acceptance is owned by the author's independent reviewer and is not re-certified here.
- **Student debt:** quoted existing-loan rate uses the same actor/currency debt identity that `common.borrow` actually reuses, including a zero-principal retained line. A same-EUR move therefore preserves its prior contract rate; a newly created debt uses the current country's rate. Country identity is not inferred from shared EUR currency. The audit found no tuition/loan unit mismatch in the inspected path.
- **Money and travel:** country pack money is scaled once through `currencyDigits`; rational FX includes both source and target scales. Travel native accommodation is converted into the organiser's residence currency, while refunds retain the saved original quote currency. FX fees round up in minor units, including JPY. Trip location is separate from residence; ordinary home consultations/treatment, enrolling and starting work are blocked during a trip. Serious hospital care interrupts the trip through the existing return path. This is not a claim that the game implements foreign healthcare entitlements or visa law.
- **Healthcare charges:** fees originate in the patient's residence currency, convert to the actual caregiver's payment currency, and medical records retain both native cost and actual payer/currency. Adult/caregiver selection uses the authored adult age. Zero-fee public care is an explicit shared model, not an unexplained units error.
- **Teen/adult boundary:** new dates require both actual ages13–17 and below each residence country's adult age; all twelve currently configure18. Exact two-calendar-year proximity, actual family/supervisor exclusions, mutual preferences, current exclusivity and physical availability are separate guards. Existing mixed-age couples remain exclusive; adulthood conversion waits until both people reach their own adult age. Adult romance checks each participant's actual country gate. Hard-coded18 in historical teen validation matches every current pack, so no present contradiction is claimed.
