## Actual authored rules

An existing known peer may be asked on a first date when both people are13–17,
below their residence country's adult age and at most24 actual calendar months
apart by DOB. Both must be living, mutually compatible, outside close family and
teacher/guardian/supervisor authority, free of another romantic relationship and
available in the same home city. Custody, pursuit, hospital care, trips and service
assignments block a new date. The actual ordinary relationship must have50bond/
40trust and the actor needs4energy. These are explicit game rules, not verified
national teen-dating laws or a new child/adult legal framework.

One request per actual pair/year records one NPC-stream consent roll. Frozen V1
chance is `max(15,min(90,25+floor((bond+trust)/2)))`. Opening/cancelling/reviewing
never draws, saves, spends energy or consumes the annual attempt. Refusal retains
the ordinary relationship and spends neither energy nor money. Acceptance records
an agreed local walk/conversation, uses4energy and creates `teen-dating`, retaining
the original friend/classmate/acquaintance roles. It does not grant bond, trust,
happiness, money, work, qualification, housing or adult privileges. Ongoing time
together uses existing interactions rather than a new compulsory date schedule.

Typed `teenRelationships` stores the actual relation/people, date/place, V1 rules,
original known roles/genders/preferences, bond/trust, chance/roll and outcome.
An explicit end retains ordinary roles, bond/trust, history and original consent.
It works remotely and in custody, including when one person has reached18 while
their existing partner remains16–17. Refusal is distinct from ending a relationship.
Trying again after an actual ending uses factual reunion wording and the same
annual pair allowance; an active pair cannot create a second initial agreement.

Shared romantic exclusivity and labels recognise the separate teen state. A real
Best Friends agreement with that same person closes through the existing romance
ending, keeping its earlier consent. Death closes the teen record on the actual
deceased person's date. During a mixed-age interval, the existing relationship
remains exclusive and can be ended; no new teen proposal bypasses the age range.
At the first normally processed month when both are adults, the record marks
continued-as-adults at that actual date and the existing relation becomes ordinary
dating. It grants no payment/date/bonus, draws nothing and adds no backdated adult
NPC courtship months. Existing adult dating/proposal/wedding/conception gates
remain intact. Profile displays a factual relationship label for both states.

## Durable Person review

The actual Person screen mounts a separate First Relationships card. It binds the
review to real life, actor, peer, relationship, place, relevant ages/year and current
agreement/history; unrelated revisions do not discard a draft. Confirmation
rechecks the actual current eligibility. Immediate submission/cancel guards and
the store's serialized save boundary prevent duplicate actions. A failed save
keeps the same review and one inline live alert. Successful saving closes it and
publishes the actual saved result. The mixed-age ending action stays reachable.
No shared EventModal sizing/semantics or existing adult card is redesigned.

Validation indexes relationships/parents/history once, has an empty-history fast
path and shares one-active-person checks with adult romance validation. Historical
consent uses its saved date/preferences instead of today's mutable preferences or
presence. Immutable histories reuse a cached index; mutable copies cannot retain
a stale active map. Malformed consent, unknown records, overlap, forged endings,
missing annual guards and unbacked teen states fail validation.
