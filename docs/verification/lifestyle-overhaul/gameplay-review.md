# Gameplay and implementation review

The implementation expands the existing Assets, Pets, Hobbies and Entertainment
routes. Existing cards, feature boxes, ActionRows, confirmation/result modals and
navigation remain in use. The specialty catalogue and new companion portraits
use two generated atlases referenced to the original art style; original images
remain unchanged. There is no new minigame in this phase requiring animation.
The previous Exit Shuffle animations and saved sessions remain supported.

## Property

A home can remain an ordinary financial asset or be explicitly reviewed for
management. Twelve paid installations add 40% of their cost as estimated value;
legacy renovation retains its original semantics, while managed renovation caps
added value below the expense. There is no repeated installation or appraisal
loop. Built-in amenities count as present. Dedicated rooms must leave space for
the actual household, and future moves into managed homes respect occupancy.
Existing households and pets are never removed by the new eligibility rules.

Named applications create actual adult NPCs, accounts and employment records.
Acceptance requires available funds and transfers a real deposit into separate
escrow. Rent starts in a later month, debits the tenant and credits the owner
once. Existing financial simulation includes those people. The player's own
rental-home lease is a separate system. Advertised rent stays in market bounds;
annual increases cannot exceed 10%. Applications have a three-month interval.

Arrears and maintenance persist. Inspection respects location and a six-month
privacy interval. Discussing arrears collects only available tenant cash.
Ordinary endings need notice and repairs; eviction additionally requires two
rents in arrears, a paid review and another month. Deposits return in full once,
including before estate settlement. A sale, foreclosure or title transfer cannot
leave an orphaned agreement or quietly assign a new landlord. Anonymous legacy
rentals keep their old behaviour until the original arrangement ends and the
player explicitly enables management.

Gatherings use real contacts, invitations, accepted attendance, costs and saved
memories. They are optional and limited to one per six months. A party can cause
actual condition damage. Home amenities support appropriate project work,
reviewed servicing or celebration costs; they do not passively award skills.

## Vehicles

Ownership and licensed use are distinct. Three original awareness quizzes save
answers, paid failures and passes, with authored country ages and bounded retries.
An old car is never assigned a fictional historical licence. Vehicle activity is
reviewed separately; later trips accumulate actual distance from the listing
reading. Newly enabled boat and aircraft distances use their own versioned
policy. Earlier saved policies retain their original distances and costs.

Trips cost money, use energy and cause wear; occasional breakdowns record their
actual roll. Service and repair improve condition without resetting birth/age or
mileage. Cosmetics are paid changes; the three upgrades can each be fitted once. A gift needs an
available consenting adult and transfers the same titled vehicle. Trade-in uses
actual sale proceeds, fee and replacement purchase; scrapping pays only salvage.

The original stolen-vehicle provenance remains authoritative. Held stolen
vehicles cannot be used as lawful property, sold, gifted, insured or inherited.
The six new boats and aircraft are leisure assets; no aviation career or amateur
racing subsystem is added. The optional racing suggestion was not necessary for
the core ownership journey. Existing compact feature boxes retain their design;
new recorded mileage replaces the listing reading only after review.

## Pets

Nine species and sixteen profiles vary in age, breed, route, fee and fixed trait.
The original six offer IDs remain available. Prospective pricing revisions keep
earlier accepted adoption fees literal. Horses need actual suitable grounds at
adoption; introducing the rule never discards an existing animal.

Training gains and tricks persist and have species limits. Small habitat animals
cannot be forced into dog activities. Personality affects actual training and
situations. Basic feeding and care remain automatic; optional interactions do
not become compulsory annual chores. Paid additional treatment records an actual
outcome. Ageing support improves comfort rather than curing ageing or resetting
birth dates. Earlier companion health policies remain literal when their later
optional revision is absent.

Family members can build distinct bonds with the same pet. Introductions save
the actual other animal and outcome; refused invitations do not become attendance.
Existing boarding, transport, illness, grief, rehoming, owner death and adult
family succession continue to use the original pet histories. Ended pets receive
no later care charges.

## Hobbies and leisure

The original eight hobby histories expand to sixteen kinds. Both paid access
modes and the two-routine limit remain. The plan's possible third routine is
optional; retaining two avoids changing existing attendance/energy balance.
Milestone labels describe actual earned proficiency and create no qualification.
Coding supplies a bounded existing Smarts benefit; guitar retains its existing
Music contribution. Singing does not duplicate Music credit, and career music
training takes precedence over overlapping hobby practice.

Formal competitions require actual practice, an earned threshold and a real
peer. Entry, consent, strategy, chance/roll and any prize persist. New competitions
and the existing community event share the same annual opportunity. A personal
project needs four genuinely attended later months. One review can credit up to
four such months, avoiding a forced four-year chain of taps. The completed work
is a saved accomplishment, without an invented saleable asset.

Shared practice saves accepted participation and relationship memories without
duplicate proficiency or career rewards. New memories retain earlier relationship
history. Ten outing types include a free park visit. Safe casual activities can
be enjoyed without property, a vehicle, paid equipment or a routine; they do not
fabricate formal practice. Children retain responsible-payer consent and appropriate
age/supervision rules.

## Situations and scope

The sixty original premises comprise 12 property, 12 vehicle, 18 pet and 18 hobby
situations. The writing inventory records every choice and operation. At most
two opportunities per person/year and a five-year template cooldown prevent a
constant management queue. Subject, participant, location, tenancy, currency and
prices bind before presentation; relevant new contexts also freeze condition,
value or training. Changed circumstances cancel without spending. Old contexts
and accepted operations retain their original interpretation.

The parrot repeats an ordinary phrase rather than an unrecorded private secret.
Informal hobby challenges use earned proficiency, chosen strategy and one saved
draw. Insurance loss, health injury and reported tenant damage use actual asset,
health and legal records. No routine vehicle action introduces disproportionate
lethality. All new functionality is free; purchase/access and RevenueCat modules
remain outside this phase.

These findings come from source review, controlled eligibility tests, actual
persistent command journeys and visual inspection of the two generated atlases.
They do not establish a numerical gameplay-quality score, human editorial
acceptance, native touch feel, screen-reader delivery or installed-device layout.
