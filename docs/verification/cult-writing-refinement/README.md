# Cult voice refinement

Follow-up to the editorial pass at 94c5020. This addresses the four review points: repetitive admissions, restrained humour, repeated outcome summaries and anonymous routine reactions.

## Changes

None of the 18 admissions now opens with “You admit”, down from 17. One still uses the phrase within the scene. Openings use direct speech, the failed promise, a follower’s response or the player coming clean. The memorial-garden apology stays serious.

All 57 routine branches were reviewed: 15 recruitment outcomes, 24 sermon outcomes and 18 ceremony outcomes. A new scene uses an existing present recruiter, adviser, treasurer or organiser, falling back to another present member. It never creates a person or consumes a random draw for writing. All 19 ordinary outcome families have at least three readings across the five existing personalities.

Recruitment includes the actual signup count and any joining fees once in the scene. Empty drives no longer end with a second report of zero followers and zero fees. Ceremony costs appear in the scene; actual positive contributions remain explicit. The repeated financial footer is gone. Existing effect rows, quotes, ledger transactions and capacity warnings are unchanged.

The sharper scenes include a toaster nominated to replace the kettle oracle, compensation demands for puberty, a hamster incarnation and a leader losing control of their own conga procession. Grief and unmet support keep a plain voice. No joke adds a payment, persistent relationship, promotion, injury, death or new gameplay outcome.

## Routine examples

These are engine-generated results from identical declared contexts. Names, counts and amounts below are the actual bound values.

### recruit:street:strong

**Before:** You get people to stop and listen. One wants to know about dental care. You steer the conversation back to your beliefs.

6 new followers joined; $240.00 in joining fees reached the treasury.

**After:** Your pavement sermon draws 6 new followers and $240.00 in joining fees. Taylor Reed has to explain that eternal life does not include dental cover.

### recruit:social:empty

**Before:** Your clip gets comments, arguments and an unsolicited haircut suggestion. It gets no recruits.

0 new followers joined; $0.00 in joining fees reached the treasury.

**After:** Nobody signs up. Taylor Reed reads you the top comment: “Is this a cult or a hostage video?”

### recruit:open-evening:modest

**Before:** You get a signed form. Other visitors thank you for the refreshments with the firm politeness of people refusing a second date.

4 new followers joined; $160.00 in joining fees reached the treasury.

**After:** Your talk and refreshments bring in 4 new followers and $160.00 in joining fees. Taylor Reed suggests testing which part works by letting you give the next talk without biscuits.

### recruit:referral:modest

**Before:** A guest fills in a form. Conversations at the table become much more careful once the membership pitch starts.

1 new follower joined; $40.00 in joining fees reached the treasury.

**After:** The invitations bring in 1 new follower and $40.00 in joining fees. Taylor Reed has to explain that your followers were real people before they met you.

### recruit:publicity:empty

**Before:** You attract cameras, spectators and no recruits. People enjoy watching you without wanting to become your responsibility.

0 new followers joined; $0.00 in joining fees reached the treasury.

**After:** The crowd films everything and nobody joins. Taylor Reed quietly takes your name off the next sign.

### preach:final-update:engaged

**Before:** You promise that reality can improve. Followers shout suggestions until someone demands a version without your longer speeches.

**After:** You promise a better version of reality. Taylor Reed demands the removal of back pain, mosquitoes and the need to wear trousers. The room gives you an impossible list.

### preach:cosmic-accounting:engaged

**Before:** You declare that the universe owes everyone. Followers cheer and begin listing what they intend to collect. You struggle to regain the floor.

**After:** You declare that the universe owes everyone. Sam Reed demands compensation for puberty. The room cheers while you search for a less expensive revelation.

### preach:ordinary-miracles:engaged

**Before:** You ask for stories of small miracles. Followers compete to tell theirs until you have a queue of people insisting theirs sounds least believable.

**After:** You ask for small miracles. Taylor Reed describes a public toilet with paper, soap and a working lock. The room reacts as if the sea has parted.

### ceremony:dream-circle:outstanding

**Before:** You get the room comparing dreams until a usually silent follower has everyone listening. They start another account before you can close the circle.

The event cost $80.00; voluntary contributions were $8.00.

**After:** At the $80.00 dream circle, Jordan Ellis reenacts a dream in which you were their pet hamster. Everyone wants the hamster’s side of the story. The collection brings in $8.00.

### ceremony:quiet-table:ordinary

**Before:** You sit through a peaceful silence, broken mainly by the chairs. Followers leave looking less exhausted.

The event cost $60.00; voluntary contributions were $6.40.

**After:** Your $60.00 quiet gathering settles down. Jordan Ellis loses a battle with a squeaky chair and has to sit very still. Members leave voluntary contributions of $6.40.

### ceremony:community-supper:failure

**Before:** You run late and short of food. Guests ask for a second helping and receive your increasingly careful apology.

The event cost $180.00; voluntary contributions were $0.00.

**After:** The $180.00 supper runs late and short. Jordan Ellis has to tell the queue that your apology is the only thing available for seconds.

### ceremony:grand-promise:outstanding

**Before:** You deliver the big gathering you advertised. Guests find seats, the programme holds together and followers cheer without waiting for your cue.

The event cost $480.00; voluntary contributions were $8.00.

**After:** Your $480.00 gathering ends with Jordan Ellis leading a conga line chanting your title. You join at the back and lose all control of your own procession. The collection reaches $8.00.
