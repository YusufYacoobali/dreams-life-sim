# Tuition scholarships

Introduced in world 25 / engine 24 / content 3. Database representation remains
four. These are fictional institutional tuition waivers, shared across the
twelve authored profiles. They do not model national aid schemes or tax relief.

## Applications and acceptance

| Award | Entry grade | Tuition waived | Renewal grade | Resource condition |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Academic Scholarship | 80% | 75% | 70% | None |
| Access Scholarship | 60% | 100% | 55% | Resources at most one annual tuition bill |

Resources are the greater of zero, actual net worth and total owned cash,
converted to residence currency using saved rational FX. Converting cash,
buying an asset or borrowing cash cannot simply hide resources. Assessment
includes locked cash and happens at access application, acceptance and renewal.
Later changes do not claw back an earlier waiver.

Choose a program and subject before applying. Admission prerequisites still
apply: secondary qualification, grades, degree for professional study, and
full-time work/study conflicts. These awards apply before a new course; they
do not retrofit an enrolled course. Application is free and consumes one saved
outcome-stream draw (academic 85%, access 65%). Each award/course/subject
application is limited per calendar year. Only one offered/active award per
person is allowed. Browsing and previewing consume nothing.

An offer lasts twelve calendar months and retains its original person,
subject, program and country/city. Explicitly accept it through Education.
Course admission is a separate draw: rejection spends no tuition and leaves
the offer available until expiry. Enrolling without the offer closes it unused.
Declining does not reset the yearly allowance.

## Actual bills and study

The institution waives part of the tuition bill, rounding down in local minor
units. Available cash or a student loan covers the exact remainder. A full
waiver draws no loan and charges no cash even if loan funding was selected.
Earlier tuition, existing loans, interest and living costs remain unchanged.
There is no grant deposit or refund.

Each supported tuition period saves its date, gross tuition, waived amount,
billed remainder, actual grades/resources, funding choice and payment/loan
ledger references where money was owed. Zero bills have no invented financial
transaction. Enrolment starts with zero completed study months.

Renewal happens when the next twelve attended study months need funding.
Hospital care, custody/pursuit, holidays and assignments pause ordinary
attendance. One-month holidays can fit around study; extended travel requires
leaving full-time study under existing travel rules. Absence grants no extra
tuition period or qualification.

Failed renewal saves the actual grade/resource assessment and ends future aid.
Loan funding then covers the full bill; unaffordable cash funding pauses the
course with completed work preserved. Past waivers remain. There is no automatic
reinstatement or reuse of the old offer. Withdrawal, expiry, relocation, death,
successful completion and failed completion remain explicit outcomes.
Existing-child legacy retains the parent's record without transferring aid.
