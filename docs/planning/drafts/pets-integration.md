# Next pet-care implementation

The original seven-file archive has been inspected outside application source.
It remains unintegrated and unverified. Adapt it after the content-four checkpoint;
do not copy the old schema assumptions or call its illustrated atlas a working
pet system. The existing genuine content-three scholarship archive protects
current/prior tuition and RNG; also preserve an actual content-four first-year
choice/result before changing world schema.

The draft supports four species/six adoption archetypes, a responsible adult for
childhood adoption, explicit permission, actual adoption/food/boarding/vet costs,
monthly care, affection, treatment, death, rehoming and ownership history. Retain
the useful principle that automatic care does not demand repetitive manual taps.

Before integration, bind the reviewed responsible adult and cost in commands so
a changed parent cannot silently become the payer. Validate all adoption,
monthly care, visit, location and ownership records against real people, dates,
currencies and ledger entries. A no-money failure must not invent a grant, loan,
refund, adoption or veterinary success. Saved draws cannot reroll on retry/import.

Monthly financial actors must include actual pet owners even after leaving the
player's household. Settle their real income/living costs before pet care without
double settlement. Pause in-person interaction for travel/custody/hospital/away
assignments; use existing available carers or genuinely funded boarding. Retain
the history of unfunded periods and any resulting shelter rehoming.

Review death/guardian ordering against actual family/estate cleanup. An existing
available local adult can take responsibility with an explicit ownership record;
minor legacy does not become an adult owner or acquire spendable trust cash.
No caregiver means a recorded shelter outcome. Death/rehoming must not leave a
stale decision, duplicate result, revived animal or sale/inheritance value.

Permanent moves need a reviewed, actual transport cost and recorded pet location
history. Preserve old human-only quotes; zero pets in earlier worlds must not
invent retrospective charges. An owner left behind keeps their animals. A paid
owner move must not teleport them or silently change the responsible payer.

Inspect the preserved atlas before registering/cropping it. Reuse original art
only after alpha/spacing/light-dark/small-size review. Add actual adoption/list/
profile/care surfaces, parent identity/cost confirmation and honest availability.
Use distinct illustration IDs where the shared registry could collide.

money/food/boarding, care illness/recovery and loss, household changes, trips,
