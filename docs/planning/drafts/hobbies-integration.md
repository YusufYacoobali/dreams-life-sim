# Original hobby/entertainment integration contract

The first four pursuits/four outings are now adapted into world 27; current
verification and remaining reference breadth are in [LEISURE.md](../../LEISURE.md).
The pre-integration contract below is retained as background, not a claim that
all its release/native gates have passed.

This is an unintegrated implementation contract. No hobby/entertainment action
is counted as shipped by this document or the atlas's reserved illustrations.
Integrate after the world-26 pet regression and checkpoint. Preserve a genuine
pet adoption/current monthly checkpoint with the unmodified committed source
before the next schema migration.

## Scope and ordinary-life purpose

Add guitar, painting, photography and chess as persistent personal pursuits.
Each retains its actual enrolment/plan, original payer/location, practice credits,
0–100 proficiency and attended/paused monthly history. Players can maintain a
small ongoing routine through Age Up; manual practice is optional and bounded.
Do not promise an owned instrument/camera from a fee that only pays for community
access/materials. Any actual durable equipment purchase belongs in the existing
asset ledger and ownership system.

Use community access and optional paid lessons, with visibly reviewed monthly
costs and different practice progress. A hobby cannot award a degree, employment,
portfolio income, a club membership, public followers or a professional contract
without the corresponding actual system. Guitar can contribute a small bounded
amount to the existing music skill; other hobby proficiency remains its own
saved field. Any additional cross-system skill benefit needs an explicit rule
and check, rather than a guessed proxy for an unrelated career.

Child participation begins at an authored venue age, with an available actual
co-resident parent/guardian reviewing permission and paying from their real bank.
Bind the named adult, fee, location and chosen routine in the command. Save any
consent draw and refusal without charging for an unattended month. Adults review
their own account. A birthday must not silently switch the payer. At adulthood,
pause parent-funded fees until the player reviews their own routine.

## Monthly integrity and lifecycle

Practice occurs only during real attended months, never on browse/load/import.
Check actual trips, hospital care, custody, pursuit, assignments, location,
energy/health and whether the original payer can still participate. Unaffordable
months pause practice and charge nothing, create no arrears or loan, and cannot
claim credits. A repeat annual command, save retry or cold annual cursor cannot
repeat a practice period, lesson fee or stat/skill gain. Settle real income and
living costs before optional activity budgets; never double settle financial
actors after household changes.

Record original payer, attendance/pause reason, quoted fee, actual charge/ledger
reference, proficiency before/after and actual bounded career/stat effects.
Manual practice has its own once-per-month limit and cannot substitute for paid
lesson attendance. Leaving preserves progress/history; returning requires current
terms. Death ends future practice/fees. A move closes the local arrangement and
requires a reviewed destination plan while retaining the person's earned skill.
Legacy preserves every person's existing pursuits; it does not transfer skill,
credit, fee responsibility or ownership to another person.

## Entertainment

Add cinema, concert, museum and nightclub outings with actual local prices,
age/venue policy, original payer and saved outcomes. A child outing requires the
actual available parent/guardian and permission; refusal costs nothing. Nightclub
admission and alcohol purchase are separate rules. Do not infer a universal
national nightlife law from a shared game age or offer drinking to an ineligible
person. Keep the game's venue policies explicitly authored until sourced.

An outing pays once and records an actual personal experience and bounded
happiness/stress/energy effect. It grants no travel months, qualification,
professional membership or collectible. Apply a meaningful once-per-period
limit. An outing cannot take place from prison, hospital, a trip elsewhere or an
away assignment. Browsing, backing out and failed writes remain free.
