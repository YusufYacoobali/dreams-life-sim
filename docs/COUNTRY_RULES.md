# Country models and verification

The twelve version-one profiles in `src/game/rules.ts` are explicit **simplified game models**. Their numbers are authored balance assumptions, not verified statements of current national, state, provincial or constituent-nation law. No profile has passed its final source-verification gate.

The wider country roster remains in scope. The implemented core differentiates currencies and minor units, school starting/transition/completion ages, work and retirement ages, base pay, living costs, tuition, loan interest, progressive income bands, clinical fees and estate allowance/tax/protected-share parameters. These profiles share documented family/medical game models; detailed legal and immigration systems remain pending.

Public school progression is automatic. Higher education requires recorded qualifications, admissions and funding. Trade courses take two years, first degrees four, professional law three after a degree, and professional medicine four after a degree. These deliberately shared game pathways simplify different national qualification systems. Doctor/lawyer jobs still require their corresponding completed professional qualification.

Role salaries scale from a local profile's base pay; ranks and part-time roles use explicit factors. Actual contracts accrue pay by month. Withholding is reconciled against actual gross income at the end of an Age Up cycle or at death. Tax uses marginal bands with exact integer rounding. The cycle follows the game's annual observation date; it is not an implementation of each jurisdiction's tax filing calendar.

Living costs use a local monthly baseline: one independent adult costs 100%, each additional adult 50%, and each dependent child 25%. Paying adults share the budget with deterministic minor-unit remainder allocation. Owning a primary home reduces that baseline to 45%, with property upkeep, insurance and mortgage payments charged separately. Children attending local mandatory school do not cover adult living costs. Unpaid costs create visible arrears. Loans accrue monthly capitalised interest; personal borrowing is capped at half current annual salary. Repayment reduces cash and principal together. The current pension model requires ten years of recorded work and pays 40% of average recorded annual gross earnings. National benefit, contribution, repayment, relief and pension schemes are not yet individually modelled.

Courtship and marriage use a shared adult-age gate, mutual preferences and consent. Ceremony budgets scale from local base pay. Spouses share a household but retain individual accounts and ownership; marriage records preserve starting net worth. NPC discretionary budgets consume 40%/65%/90% of positive monthly surplus according to practical/ordinary/impulsive traits. Unobserved contacts settle recorded wages, tax and living/discretionary costs once on return; this coarse background model does not reconstruct every missed promotion or event.

The current family model is deliberately shared across all twelve packs and is not verified family law. Conception needs mutually willing adult partners with complementary reproductive capacities and uses age/health/fertility chances. Pregnancy lasts nine months, with prenatal costs based on the doctor-visit parameter and a small loss risk when health is below 35. Adoption requires age 21–65, health at least 40, income, a household and a 10%-of-base-wage placement fee; these are game gates, not statutory eligibility criteria. Known NPC married couples can have children through the same persisted pregnancy/birth pipeline. Orphaned minors join a real living parent/relative/guardian household.

Divorce equalises changes in net worth since recorded marriage baselines; original wealth, recorded inheritances and outside-couple gifts are excluded from marital gains. Cash transfers first, with unpaid equalisation a zero-interest liability and an equal receivable owed to the other person. It does not conjure a bank loan. Primary care considers the requested parent and health/discipline/bond; shared care requires the same city. Support is assessed monthly at 10% of actual eligible gross receipts per dependent child, capped at 30% total. Eligible receipts include salary/pension, rent, dividends, clinical fees, creator payments, delivered sponsorships, media appearances and later owner distributions. Gifts, loans, capital-sale proceeds and prison stipends are excluded. An unbilled-income accumulator preserves receipts earned between observations and clears after each monthly assessment; an old-save migration does not invent retrospective support. Unpaid support is owed to the primary parent. Custody can be reviewed yearly and ends at adulthood/death; existing obligations remain collectible. These shared models simplify contested proceedings, property division, means tests and regional differences.

An estate settles exactly once from actual accounts, assets, holdings and debts. Default succession gives a living spouse half with descendants sharing the other half by family branch; a spouse without descendants receives all. Without a spouse, descendants receive all; living parents, then siblings, are fallbacks. No eligible heirs means a donation. A will allocates the discretionary pool in exact basis points, while each profile reserves its protected-family percentage against default shares. Deceased named beneficiaries are excluded and the surviving discretionary weights are renormalised; if none survive, default succession applies.

Estate liquidity uses stored offline valuations: ordinary asset sale discounts/fees, a 1% investment sale fee and zero-fee rational currency consolidation with final-minor-unit rounding. Outstanding internal receivables are assigned to an external buyer for 95% of recorded value; the original debtor still owes the principal to that external creditor. This explicit game-market assumption permits estate liquidity without duplicating a claim and cash. Creditors are paid in tax/mortgage/family/other order, before estate tax and heirs. Unpaid estate debt is written off rather than inherited. Estate tax uses each profile's allowance/rate on actual remaining value. Specific bequests survive only if debts/tax can be funded and the asset fits the recipient's protected/discretionary share; otherwise they are liquidated with a recorded explanation. These are authored simplifications, not verified national probate law.

Minor cash inheritance is held in trust until the local adult age. It remains part of net worth, with voluntary spending limited to available cash. Automatic necessary upkeep/insurance/mortgage costs on inherited ownership may draw trust funds. Guardians cannot use the child's trust for gifts or their own personal expenses. Legacy switches to an existing child at the unchanged world date and preserves any remaining annual months for explicit continuation. Migrating old saves does not invent retrospective estates or unknown historic ownership transfers.

Health probabilities, symptom groups, programme lengths and recovery/mortality values in `content/health.ts` are authored game mechanics, not clinical probabilities or treatment advice. Fourteen conditions include minor infections/injuries, chronic physical and mental conditions, cancer, heart disease and dependence. Known biological family history can change risk weighting; adoption does not invent genetic history. Routine protection targets the game's measles/chickenpox risks and seasonal protection targets influenza; neither cures existing illness or prevents common colds. The simplified 10-year/12-month protection periods do not model national immunisation schedules.

The doctor-visit price is local-profile data (including zero patient charges in some packs). Tests cost twice that price, specialist assessment four times, and programme fees use explicit condition factors with monthly follow-up at one sixth of the visit price. Public zero patient fees are supported by recorded provider salaries. Private fees move from the patient/caregiver to the actual provider's account and taxable clinical income; ordinary pension accrual still follows recorded employment pay. A minor's living caregiver pays clinical bills when available. Emergency care can create visible caregiver/patient living arrears, rather than silently minting funding. No insurance reimbursement or national eligibility/means-testing model is claimed.

Two hospital periods pay 50% of ordinary employment wages and pause course progress; pensions continue. This shared authored leave rule is not a national sick-pay entitlement. Illness also affects health/stress, grades, work performance, family planning and mortality. Smoking/drinking starting gates use US 21, Japan 20 and otherwise 18, with India alcohol 25 as a deliberately simplified regional baseline. No profile has passed legal/source verification. Recurring food/exercise/habit budgets scale from local living costs/pay. Country-specific clinical pathways, public/private access and regional consent/benefit laws remain release research work.

The legal profiles in `src/game/legalRules.ts` are also authored balance assumptions, not verified national criminal law. Responsibility ages are US/DE/ES/IT/JP 14, FR 13, CA/AU/BR/IN/ZA 12 and GB 10; individual playable offences may have a higher age gate. Regional sentence factors range from 80% to 100% of each offence’s authored baseline, with youth terms at half the local baseline. Bail is 1.5%–2.5% of local base annual pay, tripled for violent charges. Background-check visibility lasts 5–10 years by profile, or three years for a youth case. Historical judgments remain after visibility expires. These are shared gameplay simplifications, including within countries with substantial regional differences.

Incidents persist their victim, proceeds, restitution, evidence and detection/investigation/trial rolls. Undetected incidents receive one saved investigation opportunity after one to six months. A hearing normally occurs one month after arrest. Public defence is free; private counsel costs 3% of local base pay, an appeal 2%, and attempting corruption risks 4%. Representation and evidence modify the recorded trial test; a guilty plea reduces the term by 30%, and seeking leniency by 20%. These are fictional mechanics, not procedural or sentencing advice. Representation is an institutional service rather than an invented named human.

Bail moves actual cash into held escrow and stays in net worth; it returns on resolution/death, or is forfeited on flight. Court fines are paid from available funds, with the exact unpaid remainder recorded as zero-interest case-linked debt. An overturned conviction returns only the fine already paid and cancels the remaining principal. Restitution transfers actual money to a living victim, with any shortfall a conserved liability/receivable. The current model does not reopen a settled victim estate for a later compensation order. A person recorded as causing a victim’s death cannot inherit from that victim; innocent descendants retain their family branch. This shared rule follows recorded game facts, not a simulated civil proceeding.

Remand credit uses dated person/month records and cannot be reused for another consecutive charge. One current sentence per person accumulates actual terms, credit and served months. A new arrest never revokes the previous month’s earned wages. Existing ordinary contracts receive no pay/service in remand and end on conviction; higher education preserves attendance and paid tuition while paused. Compulsory youth schooling continues in custody. Earned pensions continue under the shared game rule. Food/accommodation and newly quoted essential clinical care are provided in custody; a medical bill already quoted before arrest keeps its saved price. Outside paid routines pause, while inherited property costs and actual obligations remain. Prisoners do not fund the home household’s adult living share. Children move to an available parent/relative/guardian without erasing pregnancy or parenthood.

Prison work pays local base annual pay divided by 2,400 per actual month served. Study can improve Smarts and award secondary equivalency after twelve months. Conduct affects parole eligibility after half the term, with one application per calendar year. One persisted appeal is available per sentence. Escape stops the served-time clock; surrender/recapture restores the same remaining sentence and leaves the escape charge for court. Aliases never replace identity or erase legal history. Prison contacts are actual same-unit people with their own charges and progression; youth/adult units are separated. Street/prison affiliations, illicit inventory, seizure, resale and dependence use real records and balances. No gang membership creates a passive salary.

Four fictional social platforms use a shared authored age-13 posting gate and age-18 creator-payment gate. These are game policies, not representations of national platform or child-consent laws. Player profiles start at zero followers/posts; discovered creators can have an explicitly stored prior-audience baseline, while their post history begins when observed. Unobserved NPC profiles retain their latest audience; tracked household/financial actors and committed collaborations receive actual posts, rather than claiming an autonomous complete social history for every contact.

Photo/message/video/travel/food/fitness/gaming/humour content has recorded views, likes, comments and follower changes. Travel and fitness topics require an actual trip/routine. A platform’s preferred topics improve content quality; authentic/polished/provocative intent changes production cost, reach and criticism risk. A fixed saved response roll gives a 2.5% baseline viral chance, with criticism at 0.8% for ordinary content or 12% for provocative content. These are authored probabilities. Six manual posts per profile/calendar year and two collaborations limit repeated immediate actions; persistent routines add one/two/four actual monthly posts while health, energy and custody permit. Three strikes suspend a profile for two months. Closing/reopening retains history and cannot bypass a suspension.

Creator payment requires an active adult profile, six posts, 1,000 followers and at least 30% public image. Payouts use actual delivered views, a local base-pay price index and platform factors of 80%/120%/150%/50% for Photogram/VideoWave/Streamline/LifeLink. At the US base index, one million Photogram views earns 320 USD. Earlier views are never paid again when creator payments are enabled. Income enters real accounts and taxable gross, with actual eligible receipts assessed for child support. Fictional brands quote six monthly periods with two disclosed topic-specific posts per period, no upfront term payment and termination after two consecutive misses. Completed monthly delivery pays once; fees/terms remain saved. Collaborations have stored consent and two actual profile responses. No real social service, advertisement, purchase or external account is contacted.

Public fame follows audience size plus recorded public/career recognition; public image and attention are distinct. Criticism changes image, partner trust and sometimes platform status. Responses use a persisted outcome. Convictions can end active brand agreements; visible records still control trusted-job eligibility. Interviews pay one actual appearance, while public events/community work have real costs and annual limits. Fame provides a small ordinary recruitment bonus, without bypassing qualifications or legal restrictions. Recent post detail is bounded at sixty per profile, with exact earlier yearly totals (including viral count, money and audience change) retained. Peak audience/fame remain factual observations. Death memorialises the deceased person’s profiles and ends future contracts; their child does not inherit their followers or identity.

The ownership slice uses game rules shared across packs: 2% property purchase fees, 3% property sale fees, 20% minimum mortgage deposits and a 40% gross-income payment ceiling. Mortgages have local-rate-derived interest and 10/20/30-year terms. Six missed payments trigger a discounted forced sale; any shortfall remains debt. These rules do not represent national repossession procedures. Rent is taxable gross income in the current model. Positive investment/asset gains are taxable; losses do not grant relief. Asset cost basis includes purchase fees and capital improvements. National exemptions, property taxes and itemised deductions remain unverified/unmodelled.

All securities, crypto names, initial FX ratios, dividends and price movements are authored offline game data, not live instruments or financial data. FX transactions use stored rational rates and a 1% fee rounded up to a destination minor unit; this prevents tiny rounding cycles from generating money. Net worth values each asset/account/holding once and subtracts each liability once in the residence currency.

Company operations use shared authored small-firm rules, not national corporate law. Five industries scale startup capital, order prices, material costs, premises and equipment to local base wages. Registration costs 2% of initial capital; each agency hire costs 1% of local base annual pay. Managed startups employ an actual qualified manager and worker, with wages beginning in future operating months. Owner operators take an actual paid job; another full-time job or course prevents that operating role. Capacity depends on available qualified people, performance, facilities, equipment condition and leadership. Price changes affect demand and margin; advertising costs recur even if the team cannot fulfil extra demand. These are deliberately simplified business models.

Company cash, equipment, liabilities and ledger are separate from personal accounts. Company wages must be funded by actual company cash; any shortfall is a contract-linked wage claim. Hospital leave pays the defined half-wage rate and contributes no production; remand contributes neither wages nor production. Fifteen percent tax applies to positive recorded operating profit. An owner distribution requires earned profit after previous distributions, pending costs, company debts and a cash reserve for fixed costs/current payroll. Dividends enter the owner's personal taxable gross and actual-receipt family-support calculation. Additional capital is an investment and cannot become distributable profit merely by being deposited. Necessary tax on a minor's actual income may use protected cash; voluntary spending stays locked.

Facilities/expansion convert 80% of their payment to equipment and record 20% as an expense. Equipment depreciates 0.5% per month; condition also deteriorates. Five locations, five facility levels, one employed manager and two worker positions per location plus one extra shared position are current management limits. Worn level-five facilities can be refurbished. Four additional agency placements are available per firm/calendar year. One month's actual salary is the severance obligation when dismissing another employee. Founders cannot dismiss themselves for severance. Latest sixty monthly operating records remain detailed; exact older annual revenue, costs, payroll, tax, profit and period counts remain archived.

The first period with unpaid obligations warns the player and pauses annual progression for planning. Three consecutive unpaid periods liquidate the company. Company debts have limited recourse to company assets; they do not silently become personal loans. Tax, wage and supplier creditors are ranked in that order, with exact proportional allocation within a priority on liquidation. Remaining deficiencies are recorded as written-off claims. Close/failure liquidation values equipment at 70%/50%; an ordinary sale values equipment plus earned goodwill. Goodwill begins only after twelve operating periods, uses positive trailing annual net profit and is capped at three times invested basis. Sale costs are 5% of asset/brand value; company cash has no sale fee. The owner receives only the residue after creditors. Positive ordinary-sale proceeds over invested basis enter personal taxable gains.

A sale preserves the identities of other current employees and records their equivalent contracts under external ownership. A closure/failure ends actual company contracts. An estate may retain a specifically bequeathed company only when an available continuing manager is employed and the estate can meet creditors, estate tax and protected shares; otherwise it sells the firm before distribution. A retained company keeps the same identity, team, cash, debts and history when inherited. A minor can own it under the existing manager, receives distributions in protected cash, and cannot voluntarily withdraw/fund/manage it. Essential manager-authorised maintenance uses company cash alone. A deceased employee's wage claim is assigned externally at 95% of its estimated recoverable value, never 95% of an unfunded face amount. The company's face liability remains owed to the external buyer. These claim/estate rules prevent wage promises from creating inheritable money.

The initial special-contract slice adds authored acting, modelling and professional-sport selection. These are game pathways, not national licensing/employment rules. Adults need the specified skill, health/fitness/appearance/Smarts and no conflicting job or full-time course. Acting/model/sport require skill 40/40/60. Selection has two attempts per path/calendar year and a talent/reputation-dependent saved roll. Acting/model contracts cover six/three calendar periods; sport covers thirty-six with twelve-period seasons. These roles cannot bypass selection through the ordinary job board, and ordinary NPC recruitment does not randomly assign them. Initial acting/model/sport annual base rates are 100%/120%/180% of local base wages; rank and existing recorded fame modify the reviewed quote.

Self-directed training develops one skill point per attended month; coached sessions develop two and cost local base annual pay divided by 1,000 per month. A child's real parent/guardian funds coaching from available cash; protected inheritance stays locked. Lack of funding pauses coached progress without creating a loan. Very low energy/high stress can produce no learning despite attendance/tuition. Hospital care, custody and pursuit pause outside training. Skills remain after stopping or changing the routine.

Contracts pay through the same monthly employment ledger, tax, support, service and pension calculations as ordinary work. Hospital leave receives the defined half-pay rate; custody/pursuit receives no salary or service. The fixed calendar term continues during absence. Projects record attendance and quality, and cannot complete without at least half the periods attended. Sport records actual attended match outcomes and can cause a persistent sprain; a significant sprain/fracture prevents match attendance. Career focus trades quality, fatigue/stress and public exposure. Completion changes recorded reputation/fame, without inventing followers. Awards require actual completed work, quality at least 80%, and the saved exceptional-response criterion; sport additionally requires ten recorded wins in twelve match opportunities. Awards are fictional authored game distinctions.

Project/season completion pauses the active player's year for planning. A finished contract ends future pay; three sport seasons share one actual employment history. Generic job promotion does not reset special-contract wages. A future contract reviews current rank/pay rather than repricing a signed contract retrospectively. Selection creates real persistent colleagues inside the committed action, with recorded external employment; opening screens never generates people or consumes randomness. Quitting/conviction cancels unfinished work and ends its actual employment. Death closes the current career and preserves earlier exit dates, earnings, projects and colleagues. The career age limits are authored at 85/75/40 for acting/modelling/sport, with actual exit when exceeded.
