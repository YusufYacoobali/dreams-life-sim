# Adult situations with existing friends and partners

Content seven adds fifteen situations and forty-five choices. Eight use an
existing adult friendship; seven use the current dating, engaged or married
partner. Both people must be adults, in the same country/city and available.
Travel, hospital, custody, wanted status, service absence and death exclude a
participant. An actively romantic relationship cannot fill the friend slot even
if its retained roles also include friendship. Ordinary acquaintances and
coworkers do not become friends just to supply a story.

Eligibility reads saved people/roles only, generating no contacts or random
draws. Binding saves exact full names, person IDs and relationship IDs before
display. Already-bound scenes keep those original participants even if a later
scene would select another available friend. Choices apply bounded existing
stat/bond effects. They do not move money, award qualifications, work service or
performance, invent purchases, create a marriage or advance time. The free walk
is a social moment; it grants no focused exercise session or fitness practice.
The existing annual selection and five-year template cooldown still apply.
