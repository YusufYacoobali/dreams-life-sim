# Household companions

World **26 / engine 25 / content 4 / database 4** adds persistent dogs, cats,
rabbits and parrots. Six shelter archetypes have authored ages, breeds and
personalities. These adoption choices and routine results do not add distinct
situational templates; the story catalogue remains 90.

From age six, an available actual parent/guardian in the same existing household
can take responsibility. Their name, adoption fee, monthly food/basic care and
possible extra boarding are reviewed before a request. The command binds that
adult; a changed parent cannot silently become the payer. Saved relationship
strength/chance/draw determine consent. Refusal adopts nothing and costs nothing;
the same offer cannot be requested again that calendar year. Adults adopt in
their own names without a consent draw. Existing available cash pays the fee;
no grant, borrowing, sale value, trust withdrawal or refund is invented. A
household can have four active pets.

## Actual care and life history

Pets retain names, species/breeds, birth/adoption dates, health, happiness,
temperament, person-specific bonds and a saved lifespan. Automatic food/basic
care runs once per actual month, after income/living costs and before NPC
optional spending. Financial actors include responsible owners after household
separation. Optional walks, play and feeding time build bounded bonds and stats;
manual taps are not needed to keep animals fed.

An available local adult owner provides care, otherwise another available adult
in that household can help. Travel, hospital care, custody, pursuit and service
assignments prevent in-person care; no available carer adds a real boarding
charge. Each period saves the original owner, location/currency, food/boarding
amounts, actual payment reference or zero-payment failure, carer, health draw and
resulting stats. Two consecutive unfunded periods end in recorded shelter
rehoming. This is an authored gameplay policy, not a claim about national law.

Illness can appear during actual care. A veterinary review binds the current
owner and fee; insufficient funds or a changed owner cannot charge anyone else
or consume a successful treatment. A visit saves payment, draw and result. A
successful illness treatment starts two funded recovery months. Failure remains
paid and ill; a later month permits a follow-up. Routine check-ups do not defeat
ageing or death. Unfunded recovery months do not grant healing.

Health decline and the saved lifespan can end a pet's life. Bonded living people
receive bounded grief effects. A recorded ending distinguishes health, age,
unfunded care, voluntary rehoming and loss of an owner without a successor.
Ended companions keep their identities/history and stop ageing or receiving
charges; no revival, inheritance cash value or rehoming refund is created.

On owner death, responsibility can pass only to an existing available local adult
from that household or immediate family. This happens before dependent-child
placement, so a newly generated human guardian cannot be silently assigned a
pet. Without an eligible adult, the shelter outcome is retained. Continuing as
an existing minor preserves the adult payer and the child's inheritance trust.

Permanent moves include actual pet transport in the reviewed human transport
quote. A saved pet move references the actual relocation, original owner/payer,
source/destination and currency/fee/ledger entry. Animals belonging to an owner
left behind stay there. Earlier human-only quotes and genuine old saves acquire
no retrospective pet costs. Household separation preserves the responsible owner.
