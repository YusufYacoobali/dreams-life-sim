# School outcomes and evening recovery

World 24 / engine 23 / content 3. These are authored game-course rules, not
verified national exam, attendance, fee or equivalency policies. Country entry
and completion ages remain unchanged; [the source audit](EDUCATION_SOURCE_AUDIT.md)
and deeper regional/stage work remain open.

## Actual school outcomes

At the configured completion age, a pupil needs grades of at least 50% and at
least twelve recorded study months. The saved outcome retains the original
school, country/city, date, grade, credits and passing requirements. A failed
outcome awards no qualification and closes that school history as unqualified.
Another birthday cannot replay the assessment. Reports, contacts, plans and the
failed result remain accessible through School Years.

Twelve recorded months are a minimum guard within the current simplified school
path, not a model of a country's entire curriculum. Separate preschool,
lower/upper/vocational stages, regional exam rules and fuller attendance mapping
are still required. Moving to another school window must not replace a failed
student's earned grades with a fresh estimate from their stats.

## Evening study

| Path | Entry | Actual attended work | Passing result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Complete Secondary Education | After school years/adult age, no secondary qualification | 24 months | 50% grades; one secondary qualification |
| Improve School Grades | Already secondary-qualified, grades below 55% | 12 months | 55% grades; original qualification retained |

These public game courses have no tuition fee, loan or spendable grant. Enrolling,
opening the screen or changing a routine gives no credits, grades or random draw.
They fit around ordinary employment. Full-time further study and emigration
require finishing or leaving the evening course first.

An attended month records its date, effort, previous grade and bounded gain:
relaxed 0, balanced 1, focused 2. Focused work uses more energy/stress. Extra
study requires an actual command, energy and the shared once-per-calendar-year
study allowance; leaving/re-enrolling cannot reset it. Extra study gives no month
credit. Hospital care, poor health, custody/pursuit, trips and service assignments
pause attendance. Ordinary public-school credits also pause in custody/pursuit
and on assignments; prison study remains its own actual recorded route.

Only all required attended months and the passing grade complete a course.
Failure, withdrawal, death, relocation and qualification through another route
remain explicit saved outcomes. A prison-study qualification closes an unfinished
secondary evening course without inventing evening credits or a second diploma.
Legacy retains the original parent's history and never transfers their credits
to the existing child. Earlier course records are browsable without advancing time.

## Migration and verification scope

World-23 saves receive empty school-outcome/course histories. People, earned
qualifications, bound decisions, payments, callbacks, annual cursors and random
streams stay unchanged. The genuine archive from `55fee6b` records an actual old
0%-grade diploma, eleven school reports and two earlier moments. Its earned
qualification survives; continuing the unqualified seventeenth-year snapshot
uses the new rules only for future progression.

Thirty-two focused domain checks and the connected real-SQLite component flow
pass. They cover twelve countries, grade/credit boundaries, actual work pay,
failure/retry, no instant credits or money, temporary absence, real travel/remand/
prison study/emigration, actual existing-child legacy, tamper rejection and
genuine migration. The UI flow branches the original archive, completes school,
fails/retries enrolment and the final awarding command, and resumes the same
course/results cold without duplicate qualifications. Strict application/test
typing passes. The clean full regression passes **938 tests / 62 suites** in
**697.17 seconds**, including twelve complete seeded lives. Fresh Android/iOS
Hermes exports (46 assets) and both current Metro source bundles pass. The
new-world sweep passes 120/120 lives across twelve countries in 2,040.171 seconds
with unchanged source hash. A real 236-command SQLite life retains all 79 historical
moments and cold-reloads the entire world exactly. Native visual, timing, country accuracy and
complete education acceptance remain open.

Scholarships and restricted tuition support are not implemented by these free
evening courses. The broader production backlog remains in [PROGRESS.md](PROGRESS.md).
