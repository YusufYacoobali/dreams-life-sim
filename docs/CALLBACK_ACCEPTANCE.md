# Childhood callback acceptance

The world-23 / engine-22 callbacks retain the original school, peer, teacher and
parent. The checks below extend the 28 checks at the `1633359` handoff. They
exercise the existing systems together; no callback or save format changed.

| Connection | Direct evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Travel | A real three-month parental trip cancels an outing while the parent is away. Returning early permits the original outing. A one-month family trip completes before its same-month callback; its return result stays ahead of the callback, and that absent school month earns no attendance. |
| Custody | Real vandalism arrests/remand of the student and classmate cancel their project at its deadline. An arrested approving parent cannot attend the outing; an available second parent is not substituted. |
| Permanent relocation | Real approved, funded household moves to France cancel the US invitation both when the child stays home and when the child moves with the parent. Original names, place, quote and history remain. |
| Teacher departure | Ending the actual teacher contract or recording their death cancels the project. School attendance creates a replacement teacher without transferring the old promise. |
| Parent household/death | Moving the named parent to a separate household or settling their real death/estate cancels an already offered outing and removes that exact queued decision. |
| Player death | Death clears outstanding callback decisions. A classmate cannot become a fabricated legacy child. |
| Existing-child legacy | After the student becomes an adult and has an actual saved child, continuation uses that same child, preserves the interrupted annual cursor and old school callback history, and never revives the childhood project. |
| Month twelve | A real project and arcade invitation due together remain behind an actual medical emergency. Declining assessment permits both callbacks once; hospital admission cancels both. Actual court hearings precede a teenage project, and conviction cancels it while acquittal retains it. Simultaneous medical/hearing/project queues also preserve priority and the final annual month. |
| Bounds | All project branches and arcade attendance report actual capped grades, bonds, trust, happiness and energy at both ends of the range. No qualification is awarded, and the arcade quote is charged once even when a benefit is capped. |
| Scheduled-save contradictions | Import rejects scheduled plans whose named parent moved household, teacher left, peer/player changed school, active identity changed or participant died. It does not silently repair or rebind the story. Temporary hospital absence remains loadable before the deadline. |
| SQLite failure and cold resume | The native-component flow retries permission and payment without extra draws or charges. A second flow fails the urgent-care transaction with two callbacks queued, verifies complete rollback, retries, loads the cancelled results cold and completes the year without an outing payment or repeat treatment. |

Age gates are preserved: primary-school children cannot commit the fourteen-plus
crime commands. The multiple-callback medical case and the teenage court cases
therefore use separate age-appropriate fixtures. Legacy cannot carry an open
childhood project into parenthood: the saved promise has already ended before
the player reaches the game's minimum parental age.

Automated callback acceptance does not establish Android/iOS interaction,
layout fidelity, picker/share behavior, balance or release performance. Those
gates remain in [OPENING_PLAN.md](OPENING_PLAN.md) and
[PERFORMANCE.md](PERFORMANCE.md).
