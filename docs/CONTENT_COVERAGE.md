# Story catalogue and remaining coverage

The source catalogue contains **50 distinct situational templates** against
the minimum of 250. Routine acknowledgements, generated job/asset offers,
milestones, school reports and renamed copies do not increase this count.
These are authored game stories; final editorial, balance and release acceptance
remain open. The implementation and automated checks do not imply user approval
of every scene or native visual acceptance.

Each situation is counted once below, even where its age range overlaps another
category.

The current batch adds 27 situations: three introductory scenes, six early-childhood
scenes, twelve primary-school scenes and six secondary-school scenes. Every branch
in the new batch has been checked through the actual engine, result, journal,
serialization and acknowledgement path. Twelve-country school fixtures verify
local contact binding without changing people or random streams while browsing.
The current full regression and production bundle results are recorded in
[PROGRESS.md](PROGRESS.md).

School stories use the current saved class, its qualified teacher and actual
classmates. They exclude unavailable contacts. Parent-dependent scenes require
a real living parent; three first-year alternatives need no parent at all.
The first year guarantees a choice. Names, school IDs and affected relationships
are bound before display and survive reload.

Choices apply bounded real stat, grade, skill and relationship changes. A grade
change can affect later university eligibility; compulsory completion and actual
qualifications remain prerequisites. Skills gained from a class activity provide
early experience, without granting professional training, employment or awards.
A classmate can become a friend while retaining the same identity and school role.
No story in this batch claims a purchase, payment, legal outcome or award that its
effects do not perform. The birthday invitation prepares a plan; it does not
claim the party already happened.

Current optional selection offers at most one situation at year-end, with a 78%
offer chance and a five-year per-template cooldown. The first year is guaranteed.
Two saved callback families are now in the handoff checkpoint: permission/attendance
for the existing arcade invitation, and a new shared-project promise/deadline.
Focused callback and real-SQLite interaction checks pass; broader acceptance cases
remain open in [HANDOFF.md](HANDOFF.md). Their follow-up steps do not inflate the
distinct-situation count. Rarity tiers, the broader pacing model, editorial metadata,
further callbacks and comprehensive system-specific coverage remain pending.

The remaining catalogue needs employment and all eleven special paths, adult
relationships, finances/ownership, health/legal situations, travel, pets/hobbies,
later-life/legacy callbacks and uncommon memorable outcomes. The existing system
decisions do not satisfy those distinct-story allocations. The overall game
estimate remains about 50%; 50/250 is a content count, not an overall score.
