# Natural famous-entrepreneur acceptance policy

Specification 13.2 Scenario C requires Birth → School → Social Media → Fame →
Business → Brand Deals → Investments → Family → Death. Existing funded-adult
social/company unit checks and ordinary work/leisure lifetime policies do not
establish this complete sequence. This separate policy adds evidence without
changing gameplay or demanding that every naturally generated life succeeds.

## Registered policy version 1

`tools/run-entrepreneur-sweep.cjs` pins World33 / engine34 / content9. It uses the
same optional country cohort, seeds900..911 in existing country order; this is
not twelve additional distinct annual-policy seeds. Each input is an ordinary
newborn, with its generated identity, biology, appearance and initial stats,
born January1,2000 in the country's first configured city.

1. Start Life and birth acknowledgement use the real game store/repository and
   a temporary on-disk SQLite database. Closing the connection and opening a new
   repository/store must recover the exact complete world and all eight tables.
2. Thereafter, use only immutable production engine commands. Exact world codec
   round trips check optional-action results, unanswered interviews, decisions,
   annual boundaries and death. The birth database stays unchanged. These later
   checks are pure-engine serialization checks, not SQLite gameplay saves,
   native restarts or physical timing.
3. From age6 choose walking, balanced diet, restful sleep, mindfulness and no
   smoking/drinking through the actual routine command. Attend actual school,
   select focused study and use annual extra study while enrolled. Seek ordinary
   part-time jobs, then administrator/retail/cleaner jobs through actual reviewed
   interviews and the visible careful response. Declined outcomes stand. Earned
   eligible retirement remains available.
4. At age13, create one Photogram profile with zero baseline followers and a
   saved weekly authentic-photo routine. Make at most six eligible original
   manual posts per calendar year. Use polished production only when actual
   spare money above six months of living costs exceeds ten production fees.
   Enable actual creator payments when eligible. Public disputes use the visible
   apology choice. No viral response or favourable outcome is required.
5. Preserve the stronger registered Fame50 gate. Start at most one managed café
   after actual Fame reaches50 and real savings cover its quoted capital/fee
   plus six months of personal living costs. Retain the actual external job.
   Company top-ups use only available personal cash above that reserve, target
   three real company operating reserves plus actual arrears, and occur at most
   once per actual month. A real company failure stands; no replacement company
   is created to manufacture success.
6. Decline brand offers before the company milestone; afterwards accept eligible
   real offers and seek at most one offer per actual allowed year. The existing
   routine delivers actual disclosed posts on each brand's real topic. Payments
   require actual completed delivery and an eligible profile; no term salary is
   paid upfront. Suspension, missed delivery and termination remain possible.
7. After company and earned brand income, buy an affordable whole-unit Grove
   broad-market fund position using at most10% of money above the personal
   reserve. The quote includes trading fees. Company cash pays none of it.
8. After investment, choose parenting through the real relationship-preferences
   command when needed. Adopt one two-year-old only with actual age21–65,
   health, household, income, funds, partner readiness and absence/custody
   eligibility. No partner consent, child or inherited identity is fabricated.
9. Continue ordinary annual choices to natural death, within a fixed150-year
   guard. Verify the actual settled estate, original profile memorialisation,
   receipts and ledger reconciliation. Net estate plus debt written off equals
   distributions, retained property/equity, charity, fees and tax; debt paid was
   already netted and must not be counted twice.

The policy never changes funds, dates, ages, stats, saved random streams or
outcomes after creation. It never inspects a stored future roll to choose an
action, retries a declined outcome, forces a viral response or regenerates a
fixture. Source hashes cover game, persistence, game store and tool before/after.
Every actual command contributes canonical bytes to a saved trace hash. Complete
failure diagnostics retain the last valid world and bounded recent trace.

## Interpretation and execution

Output separates integrity from product coverage. Every reached milestone has
its actual command index/revision/date. Missing phases and early death remain
explicit; seeing every phase in the wrong order does not complete Scenario C.
Natural Fame50/company/family failure can therefore leave all lives incomplete
without disguising the unmet acceptance goal. A command/ledger/conservation
failure fails integrity and exits unsuccessfully.

Use `node tools/run-entrepreneur-sweep.cjs --country US --output EMPTY_DIRECTORY`
for the initial authorised seed900 probe. Only the coordinator may authorise the
final `--all` twelve-country run after independent review and source freeze.
The output folder contains the unchanged birth databases, canonical per-life
command traces, per-life summaries and aggregate result. No new twelve-country
run is claimed in this preregistration.

Nine focused checks pass in3.906seconds: source/cohort pins, canonical trace
bytes, early-death/unordered goal reporting, visible decision policy without
hidden-outcome access, exact fee-aware fund quantities and solvent/insolvent
estate conservation. Syntax and whitespace checks pass. The first one-country
probe and the final twelve-country evidence remain separate execution steps.
