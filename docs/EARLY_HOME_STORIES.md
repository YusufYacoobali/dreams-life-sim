## Saved participants and presence

A physical parent scene requires an available player and actual parent in the
same existing household and home country/city. Travel, hospital care, custody,
pursuit and assignments make physical participation unavailable. Two missing
household IDs cannot count as co-residence. A scheduled parent-approved arcade
plan also cancels if its household is dissolved, keeping its original identities
and consuming no payment or RNG during cancellation.

Three existing scenes explicitly permit remote parent contact: check-in messages,
receiving an old photo and the school family-history interview. Their writing
now makes the message/call explicit. The parent must still be alive and available
for contact, but can be travelling or living elsewhere. Custody, hospital care,
pursuit and assignments prevent contact. A different eligible parent can be chosen
before binding; a saved choice never silently swaps its original participant.
School scenes still require the actual local classmate/current teacher.
